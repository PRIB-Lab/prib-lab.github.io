01 / THESIS

Protein design is scaling.
Selection has to keep up.

Candidate generation is becoming cheap and abundant. Experimental capacity is not. PRIB is built for the narrowing step: identify risk early, rank candidates clearly, and reserve wet-lab effort for the sequences that justify it.

02 / SYSTEM

Reliability, upstream.

A compact screening layer between protein design and experimental commitment. The output is a decision surface, not another pile of scores.

01

Represent

Sequence, structure and experimental context become reliability-aware features.

02

Stress

Perturbation-aware analysis surfaces failure-relevant behavior that static plausibility can miss.

03

Prioritize

Candidate ranking and interpretable risk drivers support review, go and stop decisions.
