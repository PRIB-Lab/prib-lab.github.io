01 / WHY NOW

Generation got faster.
Reliability didn't.

Protein design systems are getting exceptionally good at producing candidates that look plausible. The bottleneck is moving downstream — into stability, aggregation, processability, formulation and the long tail of failure modes that are expensive to discover late.

PRIB treats those failure modes as first-class engineering signals.

01

Not another generator.

PRIB sits after design and prediction, before expensive experimental commitment.

02

We focus on latent failure pathways that static plausibility can miss.

03

A decision layer.

Rank, eliminate, explain — and spend wet-lab resources where they matter most.

03 / SIGNALS

We look for what
breaks later.

Reliability is not one metric. PRIB combines multiple failure-relevant views into a decision surface that reflects how proteins behave beyond idealized conditions.

A1

Aggregation

Self-association, hydrophobic exposure and sequence-level liabilities.

S2

Stability

Absolute and perturbation-induced movement across stability landscapes.

C3

Conformational drift

Signals of excessive lability, weak constraints and unstable state transitions.

P4

Process stress

Risk under production, handling and non-ideal environmental conditions.

L5

Chemical liabilities

Interpretable sequence motifs and context-sensitive degradation proxies.

D6

Developability risk

A practical view that connects computational signals with experimental effort.
