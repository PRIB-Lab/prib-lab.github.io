01 / THESIS

Protein design is scaling.
Selection has to keep up.

Candidate generation is moving quickly. Experimental capacity remains scarce. PRIB focuses on the narrowing step: surface risk early, rank candidates clearly and make the next experiment easier to justify.

02 / SYSTEM

Reliability, upstream.

A compact screening layer between protein design and experimental commitment. Sequence, structure and context are turned into risk signals that support candidate prioritization.
